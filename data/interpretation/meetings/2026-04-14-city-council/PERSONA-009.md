- **Threat level:** 4

- **Fact:** Under state law and City Charter, the Council may only increase or decrease the school budget's bottom-line number — it cannot direct which positions to keep, which buildings to close, or how to allocate any reductions.

- **Emotional valence:** neutral
- **Threat level:** 2

- **Fact:** Elementary enrollment dropped 23% over four years (from 1,401 to 1,080 students) while district staffing grew by 82 positions — a divergence that the district's own materials present as a structural cause of the current shortfall.

#### 7. No Savings Left to Soften the Blow
- **Fact:** The district's fund balance — its financial cushion — is described as essentially depleted, meaning there is no reserve available to phase in cuts or buy time.
- **Source:** Fiscal context / meeting background (fund balance status)
