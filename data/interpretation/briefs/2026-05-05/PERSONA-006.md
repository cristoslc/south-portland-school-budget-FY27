## Open Questions

These are the questions Tom is carrying into the room:

- **What's my actual bill?** The $257/year figure I was told in March was based on a 6% levy. The board just asked for 7%. Has the per-household number been recalculated, and is anyone going to say it out loud tonight?

- **Is the council going to hold its own guidance?** They said 3–6% in February. The board came back unanimously asking for more. Does a unanimous school board vote change anyone's mind on the council side?

- **What is the total hit, all in?** Nobody has put a single number on paper combining the school tax increase, the sewer rate hikes (three years at 22%), the police/fire bond if it passes in November, and the Mahoney bond in 2027. I want to know what a home assessed at $514,000 is actually paying in new obligations over the next three to five years.

- **What happened to the 82 positions breakdown?** The school administration promised that data to the city council back in February and never delivered it. Are we now voting on a budget that includes those positions without ever being told what they are?

- **What are the terms on the fund balance backstop?** The city's undesignated fund balance has been explicitly committed to cover the school department's FY26 deficit. Is that a loan? A transfer? What does it mean for the reserve going forward?

- **What is the actual FY26 deficit number?** The last figure I heard was $80K–$1.1M, with SPTA labor costs for hours above 15 per week still being collected. Has that final number landed?

- **Who is accountable for the $2.37 million?** A commenter cited the FY25 audit at the April 7 meeting — $2.37M spent without board authorization, $400K in forfeited grants. The administration did not rebut it. Has anyone in authority addressed this since?

- **What's the plan for FY28?** The finance director said on the record that labor contracts grow costs past 6% automatically. If 6% is mathematically untenable in steady state, what is the district actually going to do differently next year?
