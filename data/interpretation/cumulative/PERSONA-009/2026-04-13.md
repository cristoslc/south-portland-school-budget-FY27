# Cumulative Record: Dana (PERSONA-009)
## Meeting: School Board Regular Meeting -- April 13, 2026 -- 2026-04-13

### Interpretation

The reconfiguration reversal arrives in under three minutes and lands with the flatness of something the board has already agreed to in the hallway. DeAngelis says April 29th. Says it is clear the board supports the delay. Then the pledge of allegiance. Dana takes the note and moves on because the story is not the announcement — the story is Amy from Kaylor, who arrives at the mic a little while later and says, calmly, that her school is not being afforded that luxury.

Everything Dana has been building since March 2 — DeAngelis's "families west of the Dairy Queen" speech, the equity-versus-fiscal-efficiency frame, the argument that who gets protected when hard choices are made tells you something true about a district — just got delivered from the other side of the ledger by a parent, not a board member. Amy names Kaylor's demographics without a slide deck: highest rates of renters, free and reduced lunch, children of color, multilingual learners. While the rest of the district exhales, her community does not. That is the whole season in two sentences, and it came from the public comment line.

Finance Director Ketchum gives Dana the hard close: "I really don't think so." It is the clearest on-record statement Dana has logged since the crisis began. No hedging, no future workshop pending. If Ketchum thought there was a financial path to keeping Kaylor open, she says, she would love to see it. She does not think there is one. Next to DeAngelis's lone no vote on the closure at March 30, that quote is brutal — the chair voted against something the district's own numbers person says cannot be undone.

Chloe Bartlett shaking at the mic is the footage that runs next to Amy. Third year in the district, third-grade teacher, visibly trembling. She wants to know whether next March brings the same nightmare again. Dana has two clips, back to back, and they do not need framing.

Brown Elementary's principal resigns. Ralph Baxter names the police report. The board votes 3-2 to bank the money rather than recall teachers. The institution Dana first tracked descending into certified failure on February 3 — the audit, the revolving door, Ketchum's own confession at March 23 — is still descending. April 29 is the next pressure point, and it does not resolve Kaylor.
