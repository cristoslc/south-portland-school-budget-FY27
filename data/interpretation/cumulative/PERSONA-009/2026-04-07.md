# Cumulative Record: Dana (PERSONA-009)
## Meeting: School Board Regular Meeting -- April 7, 2026 -- 2026-04-07

### Interpretation

April 7 was supposed to be the meeting where the budget went to city council and Dana filed her school board season-closer. She walked out with three stories and a recall petition she did not go in looking for.

She flagged Brian Green's mic moment on April 2 — naming board member expiration years in alphabetical order — as "the opening move of an electoral accountability story she has not yet filed." Ali Aldaman filed that story for her. A legal process is now on record with the city clerk. Recall organizers were collecting signatures in the back of the room while the board was still in session. Dana does not have to chase this one. It walked up to her.

The character arc she has been tracking since December 1 just doubled back on itself. DeAngelis — the equity-warrior chair from the inauguration speeches, the "families west of the Dairy Queen" speaker, the lone no vote on Kaler closure — is now simultaneously the board member who held the equity line when it cost something and the primary target of a community recall. Dana has been reading her as a through-line since December. The through-line now runs in two directions at once, and she has to hold both on camera.

The vice chair resignation breaking in the press while the meeting was still running is the transparency story that writes itself in a standup. A parent walked to the mic and told the room what the board had not. The chair confirmed it. Said details later. Moved on.

But the segment that stops channel surfers is the special ed classrooms, and it is the material that connects cleanest to everything she has been building since the February 3 audit. Feller described a principal wearing track shoes and carrying a radio to sprint to an understaffed life skills room multiple times a day. Then Andrew Gigler walked up and named the fiscal question underneath it: his classroom was short-staffed for nearly the entire school year, he and his colleagues covered the vacant positions with no extra pay, and nobody has told him where the unspent wages went. Dana has had the audit's material weakness, Ketchum's confession, the revolving door of finance directors. Gigler just handed her the question in its sharpest form. She needs the answer before April 14.

The budget passing 4-2 is the news peg. It is not the story.
