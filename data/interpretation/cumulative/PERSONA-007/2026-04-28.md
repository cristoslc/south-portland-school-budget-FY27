# Cumulative Record: Linda (PERSONA-007)
## Meeting: 2026-04-28-city-council -- 2026-04-28

### Interpretation

The city manager's zero recommendation is the thing I have to sit with tonight. Not because it's the final word — the council has its own majority, and the April 14 signal was real — but because it gives cover to anyone who was lukewarm in that room. "Staff recommends zero" is both a fiscal analysis and a political tool, and I've watched enough council votes this year to know how quickly a room reading becomes a 4-3 when staff weight lands on one side.

What actually moves the picture is the $1 to $1.5 million figure. Our business office is saying the real number is less than half what the city was planning around. A $1.5 million ask is substantively different from a $3 million ask even if the principle is identical. The "we can't afford this without a city-side tax increase" argument loses some of its force when the district's own estimate is on the table. I need that figure in front of the right people before May 12. The city manager is still recommending zero. But the magnitude of the city manager's concern should shift when the district's number is available. That's the conversation I intend to make happen before the parking lot session.

Councilor West's items caught me. She's not wrong to ask for them — removing $250,000 from city fund balance use and seeding a capital reserve are responsible fiscal asks for the city's own long-term health. I can't argue with that. But every dollar those items redirect is a dollar the city manager can point to when explaining there's no margin for forgiveness. She's doing the right thing for her institution. It just makes my coalition math harder in a room that will vote on May 12.

The deferral is what I can't rationalize into comfortable. The school explicitly asked the council to name a number tonight. They didn't. That means I'm still building a budget around a range, not a commitment. Forty-two teachers proposed for elimination, seventy-eight positions total — and whether we can hold anything back from that final list depends on a figure that's sitting in a session three weeks away. The May 5 council vote is one week after that. The June 9 referendum is a month after May 5. There is not a lot of slack in this timeline for a decision that hasn't yet been made.
