# Cumulative Summary: Jaylen (PERSONA-012)

### Current Understanding

As of late April 2026, Jaylen understands the South Portland school budget crisis as a compound institutional failure operating across multiple bodies, none of which holds formal accountability for the specific program cuts affecting his school. The district's structural gap has grown from the $7.2M figure cited since December to $8.4M as cited at the March 19 city council meeting. The FY27 budget passed 4-2 on April 7, following two consecutive failed votes. Seventy-two layoff notices were issued on March 18. Seven specific SPHS teaching positions have been identified publicly: Science (2), English (1), World Language/ESOL (1), Special Education case managers (2), Social Work (1), CTE (1), and Art (0.5 FTE — Hannah Schultz). The theater program ran through spring 2026 (the musical *Chicago* was performed) but has not been explicitly protected in any official district document. Co-curricular stipends at SPHS were cut by $27,630, with specific affected activities not publicly named. Evening shuttle service has been reduced to a single city loop, threatening after-school participation for students without private transportation.

The gap reflects both structural underfunding and documented internal financial mismanagement: 34 audit journal entries, approximately $400,000 in illegal grant fund transfers, and six finance directors in five years. Teachers organized Augusta advocacy independently and appear to have secured approximately $300,000 in additional state aid, demonstrating the state funding floor is not fixed. When $360,000 in potential city cost-sharing savings became available in April, the board majority voted 3-2 to hold it in contingency rather than restore positions. The Finance Director has warned that FY28 will require "careful planning" under the same 6% ceiling.

The formal approval chain — school board, city council (May 5), voters (June 9) — is legally constrained so that no body in that chain can specify which programs survive; all three can only act on a total dollar amount. Board member Dowling resigned April 6, leaving a vacancy through the June 9 referendum. No formal mechanism has been identified through which a specific program elimination can be contested.
