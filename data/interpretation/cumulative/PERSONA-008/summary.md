# Cumulative Summary: Rachel (PERSONA-008)

### Current Understanding

Rachel enters mid-April 2026 with a fully assembled picture of a restructuring process that moved from proposal to vote in roughly four months. The school board has voted to close Kaler Elementary (5-1) and restructure all remaining elementary schools into a primary (PreK–1) and intermediate (grades 2–4) model. The FY27 budget passed the board 4-2 on April 7 and advances to a city council vote on May 5 and a public referendum on June 9. The September 2026 implementation target is subject to a formal board vote on April 29, which board leadership has characterized as a "change of pace" rather than a reversal of the structural decision.

The deficit driving these changes is confirmed at $8.4 million — higher than the $7.2 million Rachel tracked through most of March. The gap was produced by a 23% elementary enrollment decline over four years, a period in which the district added 82 staff positions while losing 300 students, and financial leadership instability documented in repeat audit findings across successive years. State funding covers roughly 20% of actual district costs against an expected 55%, a structural gap outside local control.

As of April 14, families cannot be told which buildings their children will attend. Attendance zone boundaries do not exist. Transportation routes have not been modeled. The plan for IEP continuity as stated at the April 7 board meeting consisted of commitments to hold conversations. The administration acknowledged at that same meeting that community engagement had been sequenced after the structural vote rather than before it. An SPTA survey shows 92% of staff report negative or very negative morale, and the union has identified dozens of items requiring meet-and-consult or bargaining before any fall implementation.

The city council has no authority over school closure or reconfiguration decisions. That authority belongs entirely to the school board. The April 29 vote is the next critical intervention point.
