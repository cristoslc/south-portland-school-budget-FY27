Norms for School Board Members:
● Active Listening: Paying attention to what others are saying and showing that you are engaged, even
when you disagree. This includes asking clarifying questions and avoiding interrupting.
● Acknowledging Contributions: This may include recognizing others’ work, ideas, efforts and
contributions.
● Showing Empathy: Attempting to be mindful of the feelings, views, experiences and opinions of others,
even if you don’t agree with their perspective.
● Attempting to be mindful of the value of every individual, regardless of their background, experiences,
beliefs, or actions.
● Civility. Attempting to be mindful of others’ boundaries and privacy, and treating them with kindness.
● Avoiding Premature Judgment. Refraining from making judgments or assumptions about others based
on limited information or stereotypes.
