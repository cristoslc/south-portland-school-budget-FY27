New Business - Cost Centers
6.14 Consideration and action to authorize that $48,510,979 be appropriated for the total cost of funding public education from
pre-kindergarten to grade 12 as described in the Essential Programs and Services Funding Act and that $37,287,295 be raised as the
City's contribution to the total cost of funding public education from pre-kindergarten to grade 12 as described in the Essential Programs
and Services Funding Act in accordance with the Maine Revised Statutes, Title 20-A, section 15688.
Explanation: The City's contribution to the total cost of funding public education from pre-kindergarten to grade 12 as
described in the Essential Programs and Services Funding Act is the amount of money determined by state law to be the
minimum amount that the municipality must raise in order to receive the full amount of State dollars.
6.15 Consideration and action to authorize that $3,737,171 be raised and appropriated for the annual payments on debt service previously
approved by the voters for non-State-funded school construction projects and non-State-funded portions of school construction projects
in addition to the funds appropriated as the local share of the City's contribution to the total cost of funding public education from
pre-kindergarten to grade 12.
Explanation: Non-State-funded debt service is the amount of money needed for the annual payments on the City’s long-term
debt for major school construction projects that are not approved for state subsidy. The bonding of this long-term debt was
previously approved by the voters of the City as follows:
South Portland Middle School Construction in 2019.
Refunded portions of 2010 and 2012 bonds.
South Portland High School Addition and Renovation, approved by voters on November 2, 2010.

New Business 6.21:
Resolution to Amend a Prior Board Action Regarding PK-4 Configuration Timeline and Implementation Guidance
WHEREAS, On March 30, 2026, the South Portland School Board voted to close James O. Kaler Elementary School at the
conclusion of the 2025–26 school year and to reconfigure the four remaining elementary schools (Helena H. Dyer, Dora L. Small,
Frank I. Brown, and Dr. Waldo T. Skillin) by grade bands, with Dyer and Small serving pre-kindergarten through grade one and
Brown and Skillin serving grades two through four; and
WHEREAS, the Board recognizes the importance of ensuring that any grade reconfiguration is implemented thoughtfully, with a
primary focus on the academic, social, and emotional needs of students; and
WHEREAS, the Board seeks to provide additional time to support effective planning, communication, and implementation of the
elementary school reconfiguration model;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board hereby amends its prior action to require that the elementary grade
reconfiguration be implemented no later than the start of the 2027-2028 school year, in place of the previously established
timeline; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board directs the Superintendent and school administration to develop a reconfiguration
implementation plan in a manner that prioritizes the academic needs of students and is guided by the following policy objectives:

1. Minimizing disruption to students, including maintaining continuity of peer groups and learning environments where

required meet-and-consult obligations and continuity of services required under student support plans;
10. Preserving instructional time for students;
11. Supporting staff transitions with any necessary team adjustments, including minimizing involuntary displacement
where practicable; and
12. Promoting equitable outcomes for all students, including careful consideration of the impacts on historically
